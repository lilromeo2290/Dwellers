============================================================
PHASE 3 OBJECTIVE
============================================================

Transform the existing backend foundation into a real authenticated Dwellers user platform.

A visitor must be able to:

1. Visit Dwellers.
2. Create an account.
3. Select what they want to do on Dwellers.
4. Complete the appropriate registration/onboarding process.
5. Log in securely.
6. Be assigned the correct role.
7. Be taken to the correct dashboard.
8. Manage their profile.
9. Manage their professional/business information where applicable.
10. Manage their service areas where applicable.
11. Access only the features permitted to their role.

Authentication and authorization must be enforced SERVER-SIDE.

Never rely only on hiding buttons or pages in the frontend.
